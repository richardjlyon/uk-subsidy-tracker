    - Long-format `cross_scheme.parquet` schema: `year | scheme | cost_gbp | premium_gbp | generation_mwh | households_uk | methodology_version`. One row per (year, scheme).

    **§3 `## Scheme-year vs calendar-year reconciliation`** (~150 words):
    - The Apr-Mar (RO obligation year) vs CY (CfD calendar year) mismatch.
    - `latest_fully_reconciled_year` rule: most recent year present in EVERY shipped scheme's `annual_summary.parquet` where the scheme's primary cost column is non-null AND the scheme is past its publication-cutoff. Today (Phase 6, 2026-04-25): `2023` (CY 2023 for CfD; SY23 = Apr 2023–Mar 2024 for RO). 9-month overlap accepted as the partial-coverage caveat.
    - Hard-cap: `LATEST_COMPLETE_CFD_YEAR = 2025` excludes the in-progress CfD 2026 partial year from the headline-card "latest fully-reconciled scheme year" pick. Constant lives in `src/uk_subsidy_tracker/schemes/portal/__init__.py`; reviewed at next phase boundary.
